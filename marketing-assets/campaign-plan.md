# Pay It Sideways — Nimiq Mini Apps Competition Campaign

Campaign window: 2–16 September 2026 (Europe/London)

Primary goal: attract genuine first-time users and collect useful feedback before Cycle II judging.

Primary CTA: **Send one private thank-you today.**

Live app: https://pay-it-sideways.grand-sugar.workers.dev

## Story arc

1. **The idea** — paying it forward is lovely; paying it sideways thanks the people already beside us.
2. **The choice** — the recipient can keep the kindness or pass the exact gift onwards with a message of their own.
3. **The breakthrough** — no wallet address is needed to begin; one private link carries the message and optional NIM.
4. **The human prompt** — who deserves a thank-you today?
5. **The product proof** — show the real flow inside Nimiq Pay.
6. **Build in public** — ask where first-time users hesitate and improve from genuine feedback.
7. **Final invitation** — make one small act of appreciation before Cycle II closes.

### 3 — Choice

The most important part is not sending NIM. It is preserving choice.

When someone receives a Pay It Sideways link, they are not forced to forward it or add money. They decide whether to keep the kindness or pass the exact gift onwards. After keeping it, they can always begin a separate kindness of their own.

Which would you choose?

#PayItSideways #Nimiq #KindnessChain #CryptoForGood

Crypto gifting often begins with: “What is your wallet address?”

Pay It Sideways begins with the person.
